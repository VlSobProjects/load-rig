# Session note template

Copy this structure for every session note.
Fill sections 1 to 4 at the start, the rest at the end.

## Type
One of `architecture`, `implementation`, `documentation`, `investigation`, `maintenance`.

## Goal
One sentence stating what this session must achieve.

## Completion criteria
The conditions that make this session finished. Verifiable, not vague.

## Scope boundaries
What this session deliberately does not touch.

## Status
`completed` or `interrupted`.

## Result
What was actually delivered against the completion criteria.

## Decisions
Decisions made in this session, with links to any decision record they belong to.

## Open questions
Questions raised and not resolved.

## Next steps
The concrete next actions, in order.

## References
Branch, related decision records and any document changed by this session.
